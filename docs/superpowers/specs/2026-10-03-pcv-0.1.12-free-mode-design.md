# Private Conversation 0.1.12 — free mode design

Date: 2026-10-03. Status: approved by the user in conversation; awaiting written-spec review.
Origin: community request (DemodiX [CHIM]) for a mode that "will just exclude player presence"
(`tasks/feature-requests.md`). Builds on 0.1.11 group mode (`2026-10-03-pcv-0.1.11-group-mode-design.md`).

## Decisions (user)

| Topic | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Who the members are | The nearest **6** close, AI-active NPCs at activation, fixed for the scene (then group-mode rules) |
| Who opens | Earliest member named in the direction, else the player's current target if a member, else the nearest member |

## 1. Page

- A **Free scene** checkbox beside Solo reflection.
- When it is checked:
  - the NPC A–D pickers and the opener picker are hidden and disabled;
  - the player is always excluded;
  - "Other NPCs" (exclude or silent) applies to everyone who is not a member.
- ARM requires at least 2 eligible NPCs in the current roster.
- END is unchanged.
- Status shows "Current free scene: A, B and C." plus the existing dropped and last-turn lines.
- A pending free scene shows "Next: free scene (nearest six)."

## 2. Stored configuration

- **Pending free config:**
  - `enabled: true`, `scene_mode: 'pair'` (the internal name for multi-NPC scenes), `free: true`,
    `exclude_player: true`, `bystander_mode`;
  - no `actor_a`, `actor_b`, `actor_ids` or `opener` (members are chosen at activation).
- **On activation** it becomes a normal group config:
  - `actor_ids` = the chosen members (2–6), with `actor_a`/`actor_b` mirroring the first two;
  - `opener: 'auto'`;
  - `free: true` is kept for display and logs.
- `PCV_GROUP_MAX_MEMBERS` stays 4 for hand-picked groups; `PCV_FREE_MAX_MEMBERS = 6` for free scenes. Validation
  accepts up to 6 members only when `free === true`.
- Stored pair, group and solo configs are unchanged.

## 3. Activation

On the next eligible ordinary input with a pending free scene:

- **Candidates** are the NPCs that are strictly eligible (close report and fresh activity, as today's activation)
  **and** present in the input's routing snapshot. That snapshot carries each actor's `distance`.
- **Members** are the nearest 6 candidates by distance; ties are broken by catalog ID ascending. They are ordered
  nearest first.
- With fewer than 2 candidates, the scene stays pending (`scene_not_eligible`).
- Activation logs `member_count`, plus `free: true` via a new `free_scene` context flag.

## 4. Opener

1. The member named earliest in the direction (the 0.1.11 rule: full name or unique first word).
2. Otherwise **the player's current target**, if that NPC is a member.
   - **Pre-implementation check on the clone:** the field in the `plugin_player_routing_v2` snapshot that carries
     the targeted NPC (the simulator sends `listener` together with `target_mode: "direct"`). Use whichever field
     CHIM itself reads for the direct target. If none is reliable, skip this step.
3. Otherwise the nearest member (the first in member order).

`opener_source` gains `target` and `nearest`. `first` remains for hand-picked groups.

## 5. During the scene

The 0.1.11 group mode rules apply unchanged:

- the addressed member answers (CHIM tight rechat);
- only members speak;
- the 0.1.10 grace and wide checks apply to each member;
- members who leave are dropped while 2 remain;
- the witness list is exactly the members.

The player is always excluded. Members never join or rejoin after activation.

## 6. Logs

- Context flag `free_scene` (boolean) on `state.scope_staged`, `state.scope_activated` and `routing.request_prepared`.
- `opener_source` enum gains `target` and `nearest`.

## 7. Testing and release

- **Failing tests first** for:
  - free config validation (pending without members; active with 2–6 and `free`);
  - activation picking the nearest 6 from a 9-NPC snapshot (ties by ID) and waiting below 2;
  - the opener order (named, target, nearest);
  - page rendering and form posting (free checkbox; pickers ignored);
  - browser refresh (free hides pickers; ARM requires two eligible).
- **Simulator cases:**
  - free scene in a crowd of 9 (6 members, nearest first);
  - target opener;
  - named opener;
  - too few nearby.
- **Clone run:** about 30 AI calls, gaming distro stopped.
- **Release:** full suite on the merged result, build, tag `private_conversation-v0.1.11`'s successor
  `private_conversation-v0.1.12`, GitHub release, push `main`, hub message for the Mind Poisoning agent.

## Non-goals

- Live membership or latecomers.
- An adjustable cap (fixed at 6).
- Including the player in free scenes.
- Any Mind Poisoning change.
