**Date:** 2026-10-03.
**Status:** draft defaults, awaiting user approval.
**Source:** `tasks/audit-2026-10-03.md`, gameplay ideas G1 and G3–G7. G2 was dropped: the slur is SHARMAT's per-speaker drunk state.

## Ground rule (applies to every item)

PCV text adds scene context only. It never overrides an NPC's own condition or way of speaking, as injected by SHARMAT (drunk stages and other conditions) or by the profile. Any added guidance ends with: "This adds to, and never replaces, your own condition and way of speaking."

## G0: SHARMAT compatibility (findings from the E: clone, look-only)

- **Extension hook order.** CHIM includes extension hooks in `scandir` (alphabetical) order, so `aiagent_nsfw` (SHARMAT) runs before `private_conversation` at every hook. PCV registers its listener `JSON_TEMPLATE` hook in context_pre, after SHARMAT's (prerequest), so **PCV's listener choice wins**.
- **SHARMAT drunk and condition prompts** are appended to `HERIKA_PERS` in SHARMAT's context_pre for `HERIKA_NAME` at that time. PCV's speaker switch happens earlier (PCV prerequest), so the drunk state follows the NPC who actually speaks. **PCV never writes `HERIKA_PERS`** (verified by grep).
- **SHARMAT listener pin (prerequest.php:715-764).** In SHARMAT NPC-to-NPC intimate scenes it pins the reply listener to the scene partner (`AIAGENTNSFW_FORCE_SCENE_LISTENER`), but computes this for the speaker **before** PCV's opener switch.
- **Rule:**
  - PCV's listener hook honors SHARMAT's pin (enum = [partner]) when the partner is a scene member other than the speaker **and** the pin was computed for the current speaker: either PCV did not switch the speaker this request, or it is a rechat.
  - Otherwise PCV's scene rules apply. A partner outside the scene never widens the scene.
  - When SHARMAT's pin applies, G3 rotation and the G6 wrap-up sentinel step aside for that turn. The wrap-up scene end still happens.
  - Solo keeps its sentinel.
- **Phrases.** SHARMAT has no "end scene" or "wrap up" handling, so there is no collision. CHIM core's stop word is "wake up".
- **Logging.** `routing.request_prepared` gains `sharmat_listener` (boolean) when the pin was honored.

## G1: "end scene" said in game

- **Trigger:** an ordinary Standard input whose whole text (after the player prefix) is "end scene" or "end the scene". Case is ignored, and trailing punctuation or whitespace is allowed.
- **When it acts:** only if a scene is active or queued (or the stored state is stuck or unreadable, which END can now recover).
- **Effect:**
  - The same as END on the page: active and queued settings are cleared.
  - That input is not sent to the NPCs.
  - Logged as `state.scope_ended`, with action `end`, source `in_game`.
- **No scene:** the words pass through as ordinary speech.
- **Checked before activation**, so "end scene" never activates a queued scene first.

## G3: spread turns around the group

- **For** groups of 3 or more, including free scenes.
- **Tracking:** PCV records which members have spoken in the current round (a small `scene_turns.json`, keyed by config id).
- **Listener choices:** the JSON listener choices are narrowed to members who have not spoken yet this round, excluding the speaker. When everyone has spoken, a new round starts and all other members are allowed again.
- **Opener:** the opener counts as having spoken.
- **Context:** one sentence is added: "Prefer addressing someone who has not spoken yet."
- **Limits:**
  - CHIM's rechat budget still decides how many turns happen. PCV only changes who gets them.
  - A named or addressed member in a new direction still opens; G3 only affects follow-up turns.

## G4: scene card

- **Page field:** optional "Scene card" textarea, up to 300 characters (for example "Late night at the Bannered Mare. Tense. The treaty is on the table.").
- **Storage:** stored in the scene config (`card`); validated as UTF-8 with no control characters.
- **Prompt:** added to every scene turn, including rechats, as "Scene: <card>", plus the ground-rule sentence.
- **Lifetime:** cleared by END; kept in the form when re-arming.
- Applies to pairs, groups, free scenes and solo (for solo it frames the reflection).

## G5: turn length

- **Page select "Turn length":** Short, Normal (default) or Long.
  - **Short:** "Keep each reply to one or two sentences."
  - **Normal:** no instruction (today's behaviour).
  - **Long:** "You may speak at length, up to six sentences."
- **Solo** keeps its existing five-sentence guidance; the select is disabled for solo.
- Model compliance is partial. The README will say so.

## G6: wrap-up turn

- **Trigger:** an in-game input starting with "wrap up:" (case-insensitive). Example: "wrap up: they part ways at the gate".
- **Effect, when a pair, group or free scene is active:**
  - The text after the colon becomes the closing direction.
  - The opener rules pick the speaker, who is told to close the conversation with parting words.
  - The listener choice is `explicit_disable_rechat`, so no rechat follows.
  - After that reply the scene ends: `state.scope_ended`, reason `wrapped_up`.
- **v1 is one closing reply, not an exchange.** A two-sided goodbye would need a cross-request "closing" state, and CHIM's rechat pre-roll can skip turns at random. It is deferred.
- **Mind Poisoning note:** the stored line reads "(talking to explicit_disable_rechat)", as solo lines already do, so no listener is judged for that line.

## G7: free scene size

- **Page select:** "How many" (2–6, default 6) is enabled only when Free scene is ticked.
- **Storage:** `free_cap` in the pending free config; activation takes the nearest `free_cap` eligible NPCs.

- `routing.request_prepared` gains `card` (boolean), `pace` (short/normal/long) and `wrap_up` (boolean).
