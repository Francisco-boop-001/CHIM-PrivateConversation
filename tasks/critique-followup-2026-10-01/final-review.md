## Material limits and critique assessment

Migration requires quiesced old writers and matching PHP UID/install path. Failed migration can leave legacy files HTTP-readable where overrides are ignored; protect access and perform offline migration before claiming confidentiality. Coordination may create/chmod the lock file even when migration refuses. Temporary cleanup can remove state. Logger fallback is best effort, outside the Logs reader, and its I/O is outside the acquisition budget.

The critique is right that maintainability and observable gameplay matter more than additional review paperwork. An incorrect client ACK prevents the optional opinion effect; it does not by itself prevent the spoken reflection. Do not silently add an off-by-default opinion switch or rewrite the feature from attached advice. Defer performance-only heartbeat caching without measurements.
